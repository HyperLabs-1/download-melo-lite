Yes, absolutely. Melo Lite is 100% free forever. There are zero subscriptions, no paywalled bitrate tiers, no audio interruptions, and no banner advertisements. The project is maintained with passion by Hyperlabs as an open-source gift to the music community.

Where are downloaded offline songs stored on my phone? Can I export them?

All offline songs and albums are saved directly to your device's standard storage directory under Music/MeloLite/. They are saved as standard unencrypted high-quality .flac or 320kbps .mp3 files with complete embedded album art, artist tags, and synced lyrics. You can transfer them to your computer, backup to an SD card, or play them in any media player.

Can I import my playlists from Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube Music?

Yes! Melo Lite features an instant 1-click playlist importer. Simply open your Spotify or YouTube Music app, copy the public share link of any playlist or album, and paste it into the "Import" tab inside Melo Lite. The app will automatically map the tracks to lossless streams and save the playlist to your library in seconds.

Does Melo Lite support Android Auto, Bluetooth 5.4, and High-Res Codecs (LDAC / aptX)?

Yes! Melo Lite is built on Android's official MediaSession and AudioFocus APIs. It works seamlessly with Android Auto dashboards, Bluetooth steering wheel controls, lock screen media controls, and smartwatches. It transmits true lossless audio when paired with LDAC, aptX HD, and LHDC Bluetooth headphones.
